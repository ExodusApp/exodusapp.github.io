Studying and paid extras

Making and editing cards by hand, unlimited cards and daily reviews, and the full spacing scheduler are free. There is no app account. The first release's paid tier adds images on cards, review statistics and a study heatmap, and extra themes and card templates. Audio, widgets and deck import or export are not included in this release's paid extras.

Payment, cancellation and restore

Paid extras offers a yearly subscription that renews each year until cancelled, or a one-time lifetime purchase that never renews. There is no trial and no weekly or monthly plan. The purchase screen shows the store's localized price. Apple App Store or Google Play processes payment as the merchant of record.

Cancel through your store's subscription management page, available from Manage subscription in Settings. Restore purchases is available in Settings and Paid extras and asks the store for that store account's purchases. The app has no account of its own. Purchases and restores use RevenueCat independently of Help us count.

A refund or an expired yearly plan locks the extras at the next successful access check. Every deck, card and image you made stays and can be studied without limit, and existing images still display. Statistics and extra themes are hidden until paid access is restored. The free app keeps working either way.

Your content and privacy

Cards and review history are stored on your device, with device backup governed by your settings. Android device backup excludes images. Read the privacy policy for optional counting, purchase metadata and backup details.
